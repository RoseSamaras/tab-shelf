# Tab Shelf privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

Tab Shelf is a browser extension developed by Rose Samaras (rose@reaudit.io).

## What Tab Shelf stores
When you stash tabs, Tab Shelf saves each page's address, title, a text snapshot of the page, any note you add, and the summaries and digest it generates. It also stores your settings. All of this is kept only in your browser's local extension storage on your computer.

## What leaves your computer
Nothing is sent to the developer. Tab Shelf has no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking.

Page text is sent to an AI model only to create summaries and digests, and only to the model you choose in Settings:
- **Ollama or Chrome on-device AI**: runs on your computer; nothing leaves it.
- **OpenRouter, an Ollama cloud model, or another hosted server**: the page title, address and up to the word limit you set are sent to that service, under that service's own privacy policy.

Sites you mark as private are never sent to a cloud model.

## Sharing and sale
Your data is never sold, never shared with the developer or any third party other than the model you choose, and never used for advertising or creditworthiness.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual pages from the shelf at any time. Removing the extension permanently deletes everything it stored. You can export a copy first from Settings → Your data.
