# Tab Shelf privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Tab Shelf is a browser extension developed by Rose Samaras (rose@reaudit.io).

## What Tab Shelf stores
When you stash tabs, Tab Shelf saves each page's address, title, a text snapshot of the page, any note you add, and the summaries and digest it generates. It also stores your settings. All of this is kept only in your browser's local extension storage on your computer.

## What leaves your computer
With AI Wrapped off (the default), nothing is sent to the developer. Tab Shelf has no account, no analytics and no tracking.

Page text is sent to an AI model only to create summaries and digests, and only to the model you choose in Settings:
- **Ollama or Chrome on-device AI**: runs on your computer; nothing leaves it.
- **OpenRouter, an Ollama cloud model, or another hosted server**: the page title, address and up to the word limit you set are sent to that service, under that service's own privacy policy.

Sites you mark as private are never sent to a cloud model.

## AI Wrapped (optional, off by default)
When you turn AI Wrapped on, Tab Shelf reads the prompts you send and the replies you get on the AI sites you choose (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, DeepSeek, Grok). On Google only AI Mode is read, never ordinary searches. Prompts and replies are stored only in this browser and labelled on this computer.

Only labels are donated to the AI Demand Index (run by Reaudit, reaudit.io): for each prompt, the kind of request, journey stage, category and topic from fixed lists, the brands the prompt and reply named from the index's own brand list for your country, the websites the reply cited (domain only), the AI used, the month, your country and the prompt's language. No prompt or reply text, no account, no email and nothing that identifies you is sent. Two one-way codes made on this computer let the index count you once per month and ignore a prompt sent twice; they cannot be traced back to you.

In automatic mode a prompt is donated at the first midnight at least 12 hours after you asked it; until then you can change its labels, keep it private or delete it. In review mode nothing is sent until you press Donate. Health and legal questions are kept private unless you choose otherwise. Every donation has a withdrawal code kept only in this browser; withdrawing removes it from the index. Prompt and reply text is deleted from this browser 30 days after you asked; only the labels stay.

The index publishes totals only, never single donations, and hides any number fewer than 50 people are behind.

## Sharing and sale
Your data is never sold, never shared with the developer or any third party other than the model you choose, and never used for advertising or creditworthiness.

## Deleting your data
Delete individual pages from the shelf at any time. Removing the extension permanently deletes everything it stored. You can export a copy first from Settings → Your data.
